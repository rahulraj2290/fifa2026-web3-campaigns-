Pitch Strategy Guide

How to segment and approach each company type for maximum conversion
🏆 Tier 1: National Team Sponsors
HIGHEST PRIORITY
Who: LBank, Nexo, Bybit, BingX, Ant International
These have committed $100M+ to WC campaigns. They NEED distribution. Lead with reach, engagement rates, and match-day timing. Emphasize your audience is crypto-native football fans — their exact target. Ask for a rev-share or CPA deal tied to their existing promo.
🎯 Tier 2: Prediction Markets
HIGH VOLUME
Who: Polymarket, Kalshi, ADI Predictstreet, Myriad
$1.2B+ in market volume means user acquisition is their #1 KPI. Pitch traffic, qualified clicks, and referral links. Prediction platforms convert well from content pages — offer dedicated prediction-market placements, match previews with their odds embedded.
⚽ Tier 3: Fan Token Platforms
ENGAGEMENT PLAY
Who: Chiliz, Socios, all $ARG/$POR/$BRA etc. tokens
Fan tokens live and die by community engagement. Pitch co-created content: "vote on our page," prediction polls, fan token spotlight articles. They want votes and holders, not just brand exposure. Offer community-driven campaigns.
🎰 Tier 4: Crypto Betting/Casinos
HIGH BUDGET
Who: BC.Game, Cloudbet, Lucky Block, Betpanda, Sportsbet.io
These companies have massive marketing budgets for WC season. They know CPAs and pay well. Lead with your audience demographics, geo breakdown, and whether you can offer affiliate/sub-affiliate structures. They're experienced buyers — be direct with data.
🔧 Tier 5: Infrastructure / Blockchain
BRAND AWARENESS
Who: Avalanche, Chainlink, Algorand, Vechain
These are buying brand awareness, not direct conversions. Pitch editorial coverage, "FIFA runs on Avalanche" explainer content, long-form educational placement. Flat fee or sponsored content deals work best here.
